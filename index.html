About

Most health models are built for the average person. I work on AI that adapts to one person: in my PhD at CIIRC, Czech Technical University in Prague, I build agentic AI systems that learn from long-term wearable and clinical data to measure cumulative physiological strain and allostatic load.

Before that, at ELLIS Alicante, I studied how large language models handle mental health conversations and how to evaluate them.

I am also an international long-distance athlete for Spain, which is a large part of why physiological data interests me.
